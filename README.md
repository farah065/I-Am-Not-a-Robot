# I Am Not a Robot
## About the Game
The dead internet theory, once a conspiracy, has become reality; bots have invaded every corner of the internet, from mobile phones to PCs and smart devices. The more malicious ones even started sabotaging the very processes they were meant to help with. In an attempt to protect themselves from harm, humans have had to up their CPATCHA game. It's your first day on your new job. Can you get past the website's CAPTCHA and prove you aren't a robot?

I Am Not a Robot is a simple typing game created for the Video Game Design course (DMET909) at the German University in Cairo. Give it a shot on [itch.io](https://pixlfar.itch.io/i-am-not-a-robot) and see how far you can make it :)
